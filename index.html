How it works

Ultimate goal → anchors day 0. Mid horizon (M days) rolls from that date. Short horizon (S days) rolls inside each mid. Days roll inside each short.

Day N since goal is written as m.s.d — mid cycle, short cycle within it, day within short. Example with M=40, S=10: day 2004 = 51.1.4.

Mark each routine task with O done · X undone · / unable. Achievement is the share of O.

Write a one-line short mission, a one-line daily reflection, and a line of positive feelings per short cycle. Journal memos auto-tag with the day's iteration and date.

Appointments can be placed on any day — navigate with ← / → or tap Today. The Timeline (⇶) shows the chain of mid missions and their nested short missions.

Everything is stored in your browser. Export JSON to back up. No network needed after first load.
